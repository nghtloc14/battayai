The story

Basketball has been with me since a size-5 ball.

I know others are already building ways to find players and run sessions. I still chose to build Pickup Today, because what the court and life have taught me push me to do this better.

Nguyễn Hồ Tấn LộcFounder, Battay AI · Ho Chi Minh City
  1. Primary to middle school

    A size-5 ball

    I started playing in early primary school, with a size-5 ball made for small hands, and kept playing all through middle school.

    I understand players from the inside, because I am one.

  2. Around 2017

    District 8 basketball team

    I played for the District 8 basketball team. I still keep the photo of the whole team in purple in front of Phu Tho Arena.

    Organised basketball, seen from the player's side.

  3. Grade 10

    Third in my school, then a torn ligament

    In grade 10 I placed third in my school's basketball competition. Then I tore a knee ligament and needed surgery. I came back with a different game: reading the floor, controlling the tempo, shooting from further out, and keeping my handle.

    Player safety is core, not an add-on.

  4. 2020 – 2022

    Studying design, working as lead editor

    In 2020 I started graphic design at Van Lang University. During the 2021 lockdown I taught myself to edit video; a self-introduction for a communication-skills class was one of the first videos on my YouTube channel. Also from 2021, I was the lead editor for Minh Khai Obstetrics: video for TikTok, Reels and YouTube, plus design. That job paid my way through university.

    Learning by shipping real work, for a real audience.

  5. University years

    My first print job

    My first print job was for a bird’s-nest brand, including a product shoot I set up myself. It showed me how media, design and print connect, and taught me to value the other trades in the chain.

    Seeing the whole chain, from image to printed product.

  6. 2024

    Training with a team, running its media, coaching

    I trained with the SYS (Show Your Style) team and designed images and edited video for them. I learned a lot about how a team runs, how it trains, game knowledge and tactics, and took it back to the kids I coached at Eballs, first as a teaching assistant, then as an instructor. Each short basketball video I edited for the team reached 480 to 658 views on YouTube.

    Media for a team, and passing on what I learned to new players.

  7. 2025

    Lead editor at AEC, teaching myself 3D

    I was the lead editor at Au My Aquaculture Medicine (AEC), learned a lot and still keep good ties with former colleagues. There I taught myself Blender to build 3D product models; some 3D product visuals the company uses today were developed from models I built back then. I also built 3D medicine packaging to apply to a pharmaceutical company; I didn’t get that job.

    Adding 3D to my visual toolkit.

  8. Late 2025

    MSPORT, and the value of process

    The portfolio of print jobs I put together got me into MSPORT, a car dashcam company. I joined to film and edit, but spent about 80% of my time on design and 20% on filming and editing. There I learned the value of process: checks, approvals, and doing every stage right.

    Process thinking, the base of how I built the Trúc and Hà agents.

  9. Early 2026

    Battay AI begins

    The idea for Battay AI came, and I started on it that same day: the “Bắt Tay AI” channel, about putting AI to work on real jobs.

    Bắt tay means handshake: people and AI on the same job.

  10. 2026

    Working with AI, then Pickup Today

    I brought that process thinking into two agents built with Claude Code for my print-design work, and livestreamed the process of building agents on my YouTube channel. The time I win back goes into Pickup Today.

    Working with a process, even as one person.

I can film, edit and make content. But the path I chose is basketball.

I'm thinking about an ecosystem that brings real value to sport in Vietnam, starting with the game I'm closest to.
