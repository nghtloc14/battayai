The story

Basketball has been with me since a size-5 ball.

I know others are already building ways to find players and run sessions. I still chose to build Pickup Today, because what the court and life have taught me push me to do this better.

Nguyễn Hồ Tấn LộcFounder, Battay AI · Ho Chi Minh City
  1. Primary to middle school

    A size-5 ball

    I started playing in early primary school, with a size-5 ball made for small hands, and kept playing all through middle school.

    I understand players from the inside, because I am one.

  2. Around 2017

    District 8 basketball team

    I played for the District 8 basketball team. I still keep the photo of the whole team in purple in front of Phu Tho Arena.

    Organised basketball, seen from the player's side.

  3. Grade 10

    Third in my school, then a torn ligament

    In grade 10 I placed third in my school's basketball competition. Then I tore a knee ligament and needed surgery. I came back with a different game: reading the floor, controlling the tempo, shooting from further out, and keeping my handle.

    Player safety is core, not an add-on.

  4. 2021

    Lockdown, and teaching myself video

    I studied graphic design. During the lockdown months I worked on school projects and taught myself to edit video; a self-introduction for a communication-skills class was one of the first videos on my YouTube channel. Later I left university in my fourth year to find my own path.

    Learning by shipping real work.

  5. 2024

    Training with a team, running its media, coaching

    I trained with the SYS (Show Your Style) team and designed images and edited video for them. I learned a lot about how a team runs, how it trains, game knowledge and tactics, and took it back to the kids I coached at Eballs, first as a teaching assistant, then as an instructor. Each short basketball video I edited for the team reached 480 to 658 views on YouTube.

    Media for a team, and passing on what I learned to new players.

  6. 2025

    Filming and editing for MSPORT

    I filmed, edited and designed for MSPORT, a car dashcam company, on real products for real customers.

    Paid filming and editing, the base for Pickup Today's match video.

  7. Early 2026

    Battay AI begins

    The idea for Battay AI came, and I started on it that same day: the “Bắt Tay AI” channel, about putting AI to work on real jobs.

    Bắt tay means handshake: people and AI on the same job.

  8. 2026

    Working with AI, then Pickup Today

    I built agents with Claude Code that handle most of my print-design work, and livestreamed the process of building agents on my YouTube channel. The time I win back goes into Pickup Today.

    One person, working like a team.

I can film, edit and make content. But the path I chose is basketball.

I'm thinking about an ecosystem that brings real value to sport in Vietnam, starting with the game I'm closest to.
