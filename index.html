A problem I know from the court

To find a game tonight, players scroll several groups, find a post that may already be out of date, and message the host to ask if a spot is still open. Hosts track names across comments and private messages, and repost every time the count changes.

Pickup Today makes the session page the one current source. Every post, QR code and link points to it, and it always shows when it was last updated.

Today

  • Session details spread across Zalo and Facebook groups
  • "Any spots left?" answered one message at a time
  • Old posts still circulating after the session is full

With Pickup Today

  • One page per session: court, time, fee, open spots, who is in
  • The host taps −1 or "Sold out" and every link shows the new count
  • A status image with a QR code, made from the host's own court photo

What players and hosts get

A web app that opens from a link or QR code, no install. Payment and chat stay between players and the host, in the host's own group. Each line shows where it really stands.

What we measure: the share of sessions that fill, by district; players who come back; host minutes per session; and the actual cost per player who shows up. The first numbers come from sessions I host myself.

Who is building it

Pickup Today comes from the court, not from a pitch deck.

Nguyễn Hồ Tấn LộcFounder, Battay AI · Ho Chi Minh City

I have played basketball since primary school, starting with a size-5 ball. Early in high school I tore a knee ligament, had surgery and came back to the court. I changed how I play: reading the game, controlling the tempo, shooting from further out, and keeping my handle. It is also why safety matters to me in every session we run.

I worked as a basketball teaching assistant, then instructor, at Eballs. I studied graphic design, taught myself to film and edit, and left university in my fourth year to find my own path. I filmed, edited and designed for MSPORT, a dashcam company, and I now do paid remote print design.
