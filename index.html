How we work

One loop for every job: a person approves before anything ships, and every miss makes the next run better.

  1. Inputbrief, files, feedback
  2. Agent executesbuilds the work
  3. QAautomated checks, blind review
  4. Human approvalbefore anything ships
  5. Outputprint files, sites, products
  6. Learningeach miss becomes a rule

Products go through Build Studio: architect, builder and an independent reviewer, with approval gates from scope to release.
