The market

What already exists, and where Pickup Today starts.

Platforms for club management, scheduling and payments already operate in Vietnam, and many communities still live in Facebook and Zalo groups. Before writing a line of code, I looked at what players and hosts actually do.

7/12

Hosts are recruiting by hand

7 of 12 pickup posts had comments like “8 spots left”, and 7 clusters of posts were cross-posted to 2–3 groups.

15/31

Hosts invest in visuals

15 of 31 posts were found through their attached image: schedules, announcements, repeated post templates.

12/54

Court information is scattered

Of 54 named courts in Ho Chi Minh City, only 12 are confirmed by two or more sources; 31 have no known price.

6+

Prices are hard to predict

At least six pricing models: free, hourly, per entry, per shift, per player, monthly.

Source: Battay AI field research, 20–25 Sep 2026: 31 public pickup posts on Facebook, 115 court entries on OpenStreetMap, 5 court directories, and the public pages of a club-management platform.

Our approach

Stand in front of the host's channel, not in place of it. Hosts keep their Zalo and Facebook groups. Pickup Today gives them a public session page and a status image made in a minute, and every post points back to one source of truth.

The product

Every session gets its own page, always up to date.

A web app that opens from a link or QR code, no install. Chat and payment stay in the host's own group. Every line below shows where it really stands.

What we measure: the share of sessions that fill, by area; players who come back; host minutes per session; and the actual cost per player who shows up. The first numbers will come from sessions I host myself.

Vision

From one pickup game to a sports ecosystem.

My vision: pickup games that are cheaper and more professional, with a community that keeps growing. Next, a whole tournament run by operating agents. Then bring that way of working into the Battay AI ecosystem, into Vietnam's existing sports ecosystems, and on to other sports. Every stage is tested on real courts before it becomes a service.

  1. Stage 1Building

    The pickup game

    • Find and join sessions from many hosts and courts in one place
    • Host tools: spots, status images, updates in a few taps
    • Safety on every session page
    • Real cost per player, measured in real sessions
  2. Stage 2Proposed

    The professional game

    • Full-session video and purpose-cut clips, filmed on a 360 camera I fund myself
    • Agents that cut, assemble and export video
    • Player tracking and observable stats, checked by a person
    • Researching injury cover. No insurance is offered yet.
  3. Stage 3Vision

    Tournaments run by agents

    • Team registration, scheduling, brackets
    • Results, stats, video and media after every game
    • People keep the approval step for what matters
  4. Stage 4Vision

    The Battay AI ecosystem

    • More sports, connected to Vietnam's existing sports ecosystems
    • Venues, competitions, sponsorship
    • Room to grow for the people who build and contribute to the project
    • Transport, training, media
    • Tactical thinking for the community, training the next generation

The goal: grassroots sport that is easier to join, cheaper to organise and better looked after. AI comes in task by task, only where it saves a host or a player real effort.
